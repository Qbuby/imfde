About the company

Gap Inc. operates a portfolio of global apparel brands. During the nine-day 2025 holiday shopping period, its ecommerce sites handled more than 75 million shopping sessions and over 5 million orders.

Industry: Retail / ApparelVisit site

Keeping ecommerce reliable during the holiday shopping rush

Black Friday and Cyber Monday are among the biggest traffic tests of the year for retailers. At its 2025 holiday peak, Gap Inc. processed more than 1,500 online orders per minute.

At that scale, even relatively infrequent ecommerce errors can affect thousands of shoppers. Behind every shopping experience is a complex network of interconnected applications and services, from product discovery and inventory availability to shopping bags, payments and checkout.

Gap Inc.'s engineers are responsible for keeping these systems running smoothly, but diagnosing and resolving issues across multiple applications takes time. During peak shopping periods, shortening the time between detecting a problem and delivering a fix is particularly valuable.

Accelerating ecommerce issue detection and resolution

Gap Inc. uses Devin alongside its other AI agents and software engineers to proactively identify and resolve ecommerce issues.

Every day, Gap Inc. monitors its ecommerce sites for signs of customer friction and system issues. Devin investigates technical problems, identifies root causes and develops tested code fixes for engineers to review, validate and deploy.

Devin's ability to work across interconnected applications and services helps engineers pinpoint the underlying causes of issues that span multiple systems and coordinate fixes. Problems that previously might have taken days to diagnose and resolve can now be addressed much faster.

Recovering millions in sales and extending engineering capacity

Since implementing Devin, Gap Inc. estimates it has recovered millions in sales that could otherwise have been lost to downtime.

Beyond resolving individual issues, daily checks with Devin help Gap Inc.'s engineers spend less time on repetitive troubleshooting and more time strengthening the systems customers rely on every day.

During high-traffic shopping periods like Black Friday and Cyber Monday, this additional engineering capacity helps Gap Inc. accelerate fixes and keep the online shopping experience running smoothly.

"Customers shouldn't have to think about the technology behind their shopping experience. They should simply be able to find what they love and check out with confidence. When millions of people are shopping at once, even small technical issues can have an outsized impact. We're using AI capabilities like Devin to help our engineers work faster, tackle issues more efficiently and keep our digital experiences running smoothly. It's another example of our human-centered, digitally enabled approach — using technology to make things better for people."

— Sven Gerjets, Chief Technology Officer, Gap Inc.
