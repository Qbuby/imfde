How Basis ships faster with Devin cloud agents

About the company

Basis builds AI agents for accounting firms, covering client accounting, tax, audit, and advisory work. Based in New York, Basis is used by over 40% of the top 100 accounting firms and is backed by investors including Accel and GV.

Industry: Accounting TechnologyVisit site

Basis is a frontier AI startup whose agents turn a 20-hour accounting task into 30 minutes of human review. Every engineer has a microphone, and they often talk to Devin instead of typing.

The engineering team at Basis relies on Devin for its long-running tasks. Requests tagged to Devin in Slack come back shipped about 15 minutes later, and engineers talk about starting 10 Devin jobs before they go to bed.

"It's by far the best infrastructure for cloud agents. In terms of consistency of it working, not having weird setup issues. Devin is able to fully run stuff end-to-end, test stuff end-to-end."

Mitchell Troyanovsky, Co-founder at Basis
