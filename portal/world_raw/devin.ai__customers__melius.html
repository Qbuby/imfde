How Melius Ships 100 PRs a Day with a Small Team

About the company

Melius is a node-based creative canvas where humans and agents create together across AI image, video, audio, and text. Teams brief its agent from the canvas UI, Slack, CLI, or API and steer the work into production-ready assets. Founded in 2025 in New York, Melius is backed by General Catalyst.

Melius is a node-based creative canvas where humans and agents create together across AI image, video, audio, and text generation. The team is small, but it functions like a team of 100. Devin is the primary interface for cloud sessions and for spinning up parallel worktrees.

A standing Devin automation runs every morning, sweeping up pull requests left open for more than seven days, resolving their failing tests, and tagging a human to review. Melius ships on the order of 100 PRs a day with Devin.

"We're able to function as if we're a team of 100, with just eight people, because we're working with Devin."

Joowon Kim, Co-Founder at Melius
